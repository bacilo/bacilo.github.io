       - Relative luminance: L = 0.2126*R + 0.7152*G + 0.0722*B (where R/G/B are linearized sRGB)
       - Contrast ratio: (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05) where L1 >= L2

    2. Verify these critical pairs all pass 4.5:1 (AA normal text):
       - White (#ffffff) on grass bg (#2f5a1e) — expect 8.07:1
       - Cream (#f5f5dc) on grass bg (#2f5a1e) — expect 7.29:1
       - White (#ffffff) on dirt bg (#614228) — expect 9.06:1
       - White (#ffffff) on dark bg (#1a1a1a) — expect 17.40:1
       - White (#ffffff) on stone bg (#6b6b6b) — expect 5.33:1
       - Dark (#1a1a1a) on stone gray (#8b8b8b) — expect 5.11:1
       - Sky blue (#64b8d4) on dark bg (#1a1a1a) — expect 7.75:1
       - Creeper green (#55a715) on dark bg (#1a1a1a) — expect 5.74:1
       - Muted (#c8c8c8) on dark bg (#1a1a1a) — expect 12.67:1
